LEARN THESE

Verb + noun collocations

The verb that goes with each noun isn't random — learn them as pairs.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Native speakers don't think "which verb means X" — they think "which verb goes with this noun." That's exactly what cause × make trouble is about too.

EXAMPLES
  • She decided to face the challenge of running her own business.face a challenge
  • He finally achieved his ambition of becoming a pilot.achieve/realise an ambition
  • With no experience, she didn't stand a chance of winning.stand a chance
  • Even though it was new to her, she rose to the challenge.rise to a challenge
  • I'll probably never fulfil my ambition to become an internet influencer.fulfil an ambition
  • His first attempt at comedy was met with complete failure.meet with failure
  • The brand enjoyed limited success for a few years.enjoy success
  • Most entrepreneurs don't lack motivation, but they lack a plan.lack motivation
  • When the job came up, she jumped at the chance.jump at the chance
