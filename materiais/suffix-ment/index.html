ONE SUFFIX, ONE JOB

It turns an action into a thing

THE WHOLE RULE
verb + −ment = noun

Every single word on this page is a noun. No exceptions.

  • improve is something you do.I want to improve my pronunciation.
  • improvement is the thing that happened.There has been a big improvement.
Same idea, two jobs. That is all function means — not what the word means, but what it is doing in the sentence.
LEARN THESE FIRST

Twelve cards

The verb is on the front, the way the exam gives it to you. Say the noun out loud before you tap.

EASY — THREE
AGREE
agreement
when everyone accepts the same thing
They finally reached an agreement.
IMPROVE
improvement
a change that makes something better
There has been a real improvement in her marks.
PAY
payment
money given for something
The first payment is due in May.
MEDIUM — FOUR
ACHIEVE
achievement
something difficult that you managed to do
Winning the final was her greatest achievement.
Keeps the e. Compare argue → argument, which loses it.
REQUIRE
requirement
something you must have or must do
A passport is a requirement for the trip.
COMMIT
commitment
a promise that ties you to something
Training twice a day is a serious commitment.
One t, not two. committed doubles it, commitment does not.
MANAGE
management
the people who run a place, or the act of running it
The management refused to comment.
Keeps the e, like achievement.
HARD — FIVE
RESENT
resentment
a slow, quiet anger at being treated unfairly
She felt a growing resentment towards her sister.
BEWILDER
bewilderment
complete confusion
He stared at the form in bewilderment.
CONCEAL
concealment
the act of hiding something on purpose
He was accused of the concealment of evidence.
ENTITLE
entitlement
the right to have something, or the belief that you deserve it
Free healthcare is an entitlement here.
ENDORSE
endorsement
public support for a person or an idea
The campaign won the endorsement of three newspapers.
TEST ONE — THE JOB

What is the gap doing?

TWO SIGNALS, AND THAT'S IT

a, an, the, his, any before the gap → the gap is a thing. Use the noun.

to, can, will, didn't before the gap → the gap is an action. Use the verb.

SIX ITEMS
Look at the word just before the gap. Decide the job first, then pick the form.
1 — AGREEAfter three hours they could not ___ on a price.
could not comes first, so the gap is an action. After a modal you need the bare verb.
2 — AGREEAfter three hours they finally reached an ___.
an comes first, so the gap is a thing. Same idea as question 1, different job.
3 — IMPROVEI need to ___ my pronunciation before the exam.
to comes first, so the gap is an action.
4 — IMPROVEHer teacher noticed a real ___ this term.
a comes first, and real is describing it. Only a thing can be described that way.
5 — CONCEALHe did his best to ___ how angry he was.
to again, so action. The long word is tempting, but the signal says no.
6 — CONCEALHe was charged with the ___ of evidence.
the comes first and of comes after. Between those two, it can only be a thing.
